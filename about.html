— About Our School

A quiet, decades-long promise to every family

“ संस्कारयुक्त शिक्षा , लालीगुराँसको सदिच्छा ।। ”

Laligurans Batika began in 2036 BS with a single building, a handful of teachers, and a belief that children flourish when they are seen, heard and gently challenged. Today we run from Nursery through 10+2 in Management and Science — a proud member of DEEAN & PABSON.

  • Value-Based Learning — Daily moral education and mentor circles
  • Diverse Methodology — Activity-based, inquiry-led classrooms
  • Diligent Faculty — 45+ trained teachers · 1:18 class ratio
  • Individual Counselling — Academic, emotional & career guidance

What we stand for
