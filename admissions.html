Process

Four simple steps

  1. Enquiry — Visit the office or submit the form.
  2. Application — Fill the form with documents.
  3. Interaction — Short meeting with child & parents.
  4. Enrolment — Fee deposit and orientation.

Eligibility

  • Nursery: child must have completed 3 years by Baisakh 1
  • Grades 1–9: age-appropriate previous schooling required
  • Grade 11 (Science): SEE GPA 3.0+ with B in Maths & Science
  • Grade 11 (Management): SEE GPA 2.0+

Required documents

  • Birth certificate (copy)
  • Previous school transfer & mark sheet
  • 2 passport-size photos of the child
  • Parents' citizenship copies
  • Vaccination record (for ECD)

Scholarships

Up to 50% tuition support for SEE toppers, sports champions and children of single parents. Need-based aid available — speak with our principal in confidence.

— FAQs

Frequently asked questions

When do admissions open for PG–SKG?

A special admission drive for Play Group, Nursery, LKG and SKG opens on Shree Panchami (Saraswati Puja) with the traditional Akshar Aarambha ceremony.

Are Grade 1–10+2 admissions open now?

General admissions for Grade 1 through 10+2 are currently closed for this session and will re-open for the next academic year. You may still submit an enquiry so we can notify you.

What documents are required?

Birth certificate (copy), previous school transfer & mark sheet, 2 passport-size photos of the child, parents’ citizenship copies, and vaccination record for ECD.

What is the eligibility for Grade 11?

Science: SEE GPA 3.0+ with B in Maths & Science. Management: SEE GPA 2.0+.

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes — up to 50% tuition support for SEE toppers, sports champions and children of single parents. Need-based aid is available; speak with the principal in confidence.

What are the office hours?

Sunday to Saturday, 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM. Phone: 01-5149347 · Email: info@lbbss.edu.np

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes. Campus visits are welcome during office hours. Contact us to book a convenient time.
